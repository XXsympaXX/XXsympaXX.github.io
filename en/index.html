16 fighters, 16 unique traits

Each fighter has their own attacks, special abilities and a trait that changes how they play. Tap one to see how it works.

  • Aura

    The Boreal Blade

    TraitTempered Edge

    Agile knight in light armor with an energy sword. Medium reach, fast and deadly in the air.

    Hitting with the blade tip builds EDGE: spend it to cancel (Recovery, shield after contact) or, at 3, on Full Aurora (side special + shield).

  • Magno

    The Igneous Colossus

    TraitThermal Core

    Golem of volcanic rock. Slow, super heavy, with mountain-splitting blows. Super armor on his side smash.

    Fire heats him up: at 70 he is Incandescent; at 100 comes the Fissure and a forced eruption. Purge (down special + shield) cools him down.

  • Nova

    Comet Pilot

    TraitPlasma Cells

    Pilot with a jetpack and a blaster. Controls distance with projectiles and recovers from very far away.

    Missile, mine, rocket and booster spend cells, which recharge on the ground. With 4 cells, the charged shot becomes Comet Rain.

  • Kiri

    Fox of the Dunes

    TraitInstinct

    Desert fox ninja. Ultra light and fast: quick combos, shurikens and a whirlwind that takes him anywhere.

    Dodging a hit at the last moment builds INSTINCT: at 50, Sand Substitution puts him behind the rival; at 100, Dune Judgment (down special + shield).

  • Brisa

    Zephyr Dancer

    TraitChoreography

    Dances with two fans and the wind on her side. Floats, pushes with gusts and throws a fan that comes back to her hand.

    Each different move that lands adds a DANCE step; repeating one takes a step away. With 5, Greater Zephyr (down special + shield) pushes nearby rivals away.

  • Torque

    The Iron Mechanic

    TraitGearbox

    Stocky robot with an extending claw and a giant wrench. Grabs from afar, his throws finish rivals off and he hooks onto ledges.

    Down special on the ground shifts gear: 1st is the press (Hydraulic Press), 2nd is cruise, 3rd is a fast turbo with a weaker grab.

  • Umbra

    The Veiled Reaper

    TraitSoul Chain

    Shadow specter with a huge-reach scythe. Floats, fades into the shadows and sets traps.

    Flying near a rival charges ANIMA; at 100, her next scythe swing chains them: they can't get away while she stays in the air.

  • Coral

    Reef Fist

    TraitRhythm

    Amphibian boxer with mother-of-pearl gloves. Chains hits at full speed, throws bursting bubbles and counters from inside a bubble.

    Hitting on the beat (120 BPM) cuts recovery and builds a STREAK; at 3, the next on-beat hit carries the Clave and breaks through shields.

  • Voltio

    Lightning Courier

    TraitVoltage

    Electric delivery runner on skates. The fastest of the roster: stunning hits and an unstoppable lightning dash.

    Running near the rival charges VOLTAGE; each electric hit on a shield discharges 25 and goes through it. With 50, his down tilt stuns and High-Voltage Line unlocks (side special + shield).

  • Romero

    The Oak Druid

    TraitCycle of Seasons

    Old druid with a living staff. Sows roots that trap, heals by meditating and hits slowly but from very far away.

    Every 10 s the season and his moveset change: Spring heals while meditating and plants the Oak, Summer extends roots, Autumn throws leaves, Winter grants armor. Meditating speeds up the clock.

  • Brasa

    Volcano Hatchling

    TraitGrowth Spurt

    Little fire dragon with three jumps and wings to glide. Breathes a continuous stream of flames.

    The fire she deals makes her grow: agile Hatchling, Young, and heavy Adult with fire ×1.15. Volcanic Molt (down special in the air) turns her back into a Hatchling.

  • Quilla

    Anchor Captain

    TraitLoot

    Pirate with an anchor she throws and pulls back, arcing cannonballs and a grappling hook that brings her back to the ledge.

    Throws, anchors and steals earn doubloons: Broadside (2), Anchor Drop (1) and, with 5, Ocean Floor (down special + shield). Boarding (back throw) steals the rival's resource.

  • Glacia

    Frost Queen

    TraitFrost

    Defensive ice sorceress: raises walls, covers the floor in frost and freezes badly damaged rivals.

    Her hits frost the rival; at 100, the next one freezes them. Jab, forward tilt or forward smash on a frozen rival locks them in the Ice Coffin, which goes flying.

  • Mimo

    The Copycat Doll

    TraitSewing Kit

    Rag doll with giant scissors. Snips a piece of his rival to copy their neutral special. Tricky and slippery.

    His scissor snip copies the rival's neutral special into one of 2 slots with 3 uses. Neutral special + shield switches slot; with two projectiles, Double Show (down special + shield).

  • Orbe

    The Spherical Puppeteer

    TraitNode Network

    Spherical robot that splits into drones: steers one from afar while its body defends and plants turrets.

    Drone, turret, capsule and body are nodes: down special + shield fires beams between them (spends LINK). With 3 nodes and 60, Charge Triangle.

  • Titán

    The Walking Bastion

    TraitBastion Plates

    Giant armored warrior. The heaviest: his tower shield stops projectiles from the front and his charge can't be stopped.

    Three plates soften strong hits and break; each one lost makes him faster and more fragile. At 0 he is UNLEASHED. Down special: Reforge or Stand Firm.

  • Learn

    Learn the controls step by step, discover each fighter's trait and practice combos against a configurable dummy.

Controls

Touch, keyboard or controller: use whatever you have. In the game, check them under Controles (Controls).

Touch

On phones and tablets you get an on-screen joystick and buttons: Attack, Special, Jump, Shield, Grab, Emote and Pause. Best in landscape.

Keyboard

Two players can share one keyboard. Where an action has two keys, either one works.

Controller

Works with standard controllers (wired or Bluetooth). Connect up to four.

  • Stick or D-pad: move
  • Bottom or top button (A / Y): jump
  • Left button (X): attack
  • Right button (B): special
  • Triggers and LB: shield · RB: grab
  • Select: emote · Start: pause

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?
Yes, completely. There are no in-game purchases, no ads and nothing to pay for. The Star Pass is free too: its rewards are earned by playing.
Do I need an account?
No. Open the website and play. Your progress (level, Star Pass, cosmetics) is saved on your device. If you clear your browser data, it's lost.
How do I play online with friends?
Go to Online, press Crear sala (Create room) and share the 5-letter code. Your friends choose Unirse (Join) and type the code. Up to 4 players. To play people at your level, go to Clasificatoria (Ranked) and search for a match. Online play needs internet.
Does it work on phones?
Yes. It runs in the browser with touch controls, best in landscape. You can also connect a controller or keyboard. To have it like an app, press Install.
How do I install it?
On Android and in Chrome or Edge on PC, press Install on this page. On iPhone or iPad, open the site in Safari, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. Installing is optional: you can also play without installing.
Can I play offline?
Yes, after your first visit: the game is stored in your browser. Only online play and Ranked need a connection.
Is it safe for kids?
There is no free-text chat: online there are only predefined phrases and emotes, and you can mute anyone. In private rooms each player can set a nickname, which goes through a filter; in Ranked everyone sees generated names. Violence is cartoon-style: fighters fly off the stage, no blood. Read the privacy policy (in Spanish).
What language is the game in?
The game is in Spanish. This website is available in Spanish and English.
I have a question or an idea. Where do I send it?
If your question isn’t answered here or you want to suggest something, use the Idea box. Only the creator reads it.

Install Choque Estelar

Installing adds an icon to your device and opens the game full screen, even offline. It's optional.

iPhone and iPad

Open this site in Safari, tap Share (the square with an up arrow) and choose Add to Home Screen. On recent iOS it also works from other browsers, in their share menu.

Android

In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. In other browsers, look for a similar option in the menu.

PC or Mac

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar, or open the browser menu and look for Install Choque Estelar. In Safari on Mac: File → Add to Dock. Firefox for PC doesn't install websites: you can play right in the tab.
